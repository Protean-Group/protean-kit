WHAT THAT LOOKS LIKE

The connection is practical.

These are examples of how disciplines meet—not claims of client outcomes.

COMPUTE + COST

Where should the model run?

Weigh the workflow, privacy requirements, hardware capacity, and operating cost together before choosing local or hosted deployment.

SPECIFICATION + SUPPLY

Are two quotes comparable?

Check grade, tolerances, delivery responsibilities, and lead time before treating a lower unit price as a better offer.

KNOWLEDGE + OPERATIONS

Can the next person use it?

Training, documentation, and a handover matter alongside the tool. Define who will operate it and what support they need.

HOW WE WANT TO BE JUDGED
