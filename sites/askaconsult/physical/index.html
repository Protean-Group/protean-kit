PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Let’s make it clear.

Do I need a complete specification to start?

No. Bring what you have: a drawing, a sample, or a description. We can help identify the missing information. Technical requirements still need approval from the appropriate qualified people.

Is there a minimum order size?

There is no fixed minimum for a conversation. We agree the engagement around your need, from a single consignment to a standing supply arrangement. Supplier minimums depend on the product.

Where do you source from?

We work across international markets, including Asia, Europe, the Middle East, and the Americas. The search follows your specification and commercial requirements.

How are fees and delivery times agreed?

Consulting fees are agreed up front. Procurement terms are presented before commitment. Lead times depend on supplier capacity, the material, and the destination; we establish them for your specific requirement.
